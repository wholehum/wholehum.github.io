About Whole Hum

Sound for the whole you.

Whole Hum is sound for the whole you. We make frequencies, tones and calming soundscapes to help you slow down, reset and feel more like yourself. Sessions range from 432Hz and Solfeggio tones to binaural beats for calm, focus, deep rest and morning energy. Each one pairs intentional sound with gentle, slow-moving visuals.

Tune in to

  • Unwind and find inner calm
  • Meditate, breathe and stretch
  • Focus while you work or study
  • Drift into deep, restful sleep
  • Start your day grounded and clear

New sessions arrive every week, plus short resets you can take anywhere. Headphones are recommended for binaural beats.

How we make it

Sound and visuals are made with original and AI-assisted tools. Whole Hum is for relaxation and wellbeing and isn’t medical advice.

We keep our descriptions honest: we explain what’s in each session and link to sources when we talk about frequencies or research. We don’t make health claims.
